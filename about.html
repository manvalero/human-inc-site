About Human Inc.

Human Inc. grew from a question Man Valero kept encountering through much of his career: why do familiar organizational problems return even after changing people, processes, responsibilities, or tools?

Working across different companies, industries, and functions in Mexico and the United States, he kept seeing variations of the same difficulties appear in different settings. The recurrence stayed with him.

He later chose to leave corporate work to be present for his children. Away from the daily rhythm of an organization, he had room to look back at experiences that had once seemed separate.

He kept observing and writing.

Over time, the human body became the reference that helped bring the work together. Human Inc. developed into an independent body of work focused on organizational structure and the relationships that connect its different functions.

From there came Human Inc.: Structural Coherence, Human Inc. Observations, and Human Inc. Fractal Frame.

Human Inc. continues through writing, observation, and work with organizations.

Man Valero

Man Valero is the founder of Human Inc. and author of Human Inc.: Structural Coherence.

Born in Mexico City, he has spent about half of his adult life in Mexico and half in the United States. He studied Industrial Relations at Universidad Iberoamericana, and his professional path has included commercial work, research, entrepreneurship, publishing, and direct work with clients.

Today, Man brings Human Inc. into keynotes, workshops, and executive engagements.

Through Human Inc. Fractal Frame, he works with leaders and teams on consequential organizational questions, taking those questions through scale and scope to widen the possibilities they can see and consider.

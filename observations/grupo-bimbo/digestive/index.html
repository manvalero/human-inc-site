Grupo Bimbo

In 2009, Grupo Bimbo acquired Weston Foods’ U.S. fresh-bakery business for $2.38 billion, largely with debt. That year, Bimbo refinanced part of that debt and prepaid $300 million from its own resources, while U.S. operations generated strong cash flow. Bimbo continued reducing debt into 2010. That November, it announced another major acquisition. What entered was already becoming usable.

Grupo Bimbo

By January 2024, Grupo Bimbo had spent 18 months extending a new way of protecting information to 35,000 associates. The work began by distinguishing sensitive and business-critical information from the rest. That distinction determined what could be shared and under what circumstances. Protection could adjust as context changed. The response remained proportionate to what had been recognized.

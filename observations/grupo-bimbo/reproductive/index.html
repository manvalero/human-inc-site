Grupo Bimbo

In 1954, Grupo Bimbo began testing individual cakes at its Santa María plant. Two years later, it created Pabisa for cakes and pastries. Its first products fell short of expectations. In 1957, the effort continued as Marinela. Its early cakes were difficult to distribute until experimentation made them wrappable. By 1972, Marinela had a larger plant where new products were being made. What began inside Bimbo had become a place where more could begin.

Grupo Bimbo

In 2018, Grupo Bimbo reported that three million customers companywide were visited weekly, some six or seven times. A year later, the Connection Center opened in Mexico, centralizing customer and consumer contact. When social distancing restricted direct contact, Bimbo Contigo broadened support for small shopkeepers, with the Connection Center as a permanent assistance link by phone, email, and chat. Closeness continued through a different form of contact.

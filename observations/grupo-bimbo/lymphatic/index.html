Grupo Bimbo

In 2015, Grupo Bimbo established that palm-oil suppliers not meeting its requirements would first be given time to correct. By 2018, after two suppliers continued to fall short, Bimbo had stopped sourcing from them. In January 2019, Bimbo reported that its process for handling noncompliant suppliers was being formalized and shared with procurement leads. What could not continue had a clear way out.

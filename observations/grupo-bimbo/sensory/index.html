Grupo Bimbo

In 2015, Gansito Red Velvet launched in the United States. It was not planned for Mexico, where Bimbo believed consumers might resist a variation of the familiar product. Instead, Mexican consumers began asking for it online. Digital listening registered the growing conversation. That recognition led Bimbo to test Red Velvet in Mexico City and Guadalajara. Familiarity had been mistaken for perception.

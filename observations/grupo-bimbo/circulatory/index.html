Grupo Bimbo

In 2011, Grupo Bimbo improved Universidad Virtual Bimbo to encourage self-training. The university offered more than 600 courses, while completions rose from 7,741 in 2010 to 37,396 in 2011. The following year, Bimbo introduced automatic registration for some content, making needed courses easier to access. What was available became easier to reach.

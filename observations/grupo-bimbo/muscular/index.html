Grupo Bimbo

In the 1980s, Grupo Bimbo installed a new bun line in Mexico after its existing technology could not meet McDonald’s quality requirements. The line was expensive, the decision difficult, but it proved capable. That success led Bimbo to change every bun line across its system. The higher standard left greater strength in place.

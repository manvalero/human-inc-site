Muscular
NASA

For every hour astronauts spent servicing Hubble in orbit, at least ten hours were spent preparing on Earth. In underwater mock-ups and pressurized suits, body positions, tool transfers, and component replacements were rehearsed repeatedly. At Hubble, the rehearsed sequence became steady movement. Each motion held the repetitions that had formed it on the ground.

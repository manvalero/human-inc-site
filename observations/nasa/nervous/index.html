Human Inc.
Observations

Nervous
NASA

Thirty-six seconds after Apollo 12 lifted off, lightning disrupted the spacecraft’s electrical systems. The vehicle continued transmitting. On the ground, its readings no longer formed a coherent picture. The pattern was recognized at one console. The call—“SCE to AUX”—was carried through the flight director and capsule communicator to the crew. Inside the spacecraft, the corresponding switch was moved. Readable telemetry returned. Clarity returned with it.

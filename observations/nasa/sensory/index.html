NASA

In 1975, NASA introduced the worm logotype. In 1992, seeking to restore pride associated with its earlier insignia, the agency withdrew it from official use and returned to the meatball. Beyond NASA, the worm remained visible on clothing, merchandise, and throughout popular culture. Nearly three decades later, it returned to limited use as human spaceflight entered a new era. Public recognition had outlasted official use.

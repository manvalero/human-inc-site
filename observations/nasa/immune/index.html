NASA

In 1999, Mars Polar Lander fell silent during its descent. The investigation concluded that an indication produced as the landing legs deployed may have been mistaken for touchdown. The engines may have stopped while the spacecraft was still above Mars. Phoenix was later built from related landing architecture, with that defect corrected. In 2008, Phoenix descended, recognized touchdown, and landed safely. The distinction had entered the next descent.

Lymphatic
NASA

Beginning in 1981, the Space Shuttle’s solid rocket boosters separated two minutes after launch and descended under parachutes into the Atlantic. Recovery crews gathered them from the water and towed them back to Cape Canaveral. At Cape Canaveral, seawater and salt were cleared away. The boosters were disassembled, and their components entered separate routes for restoration. The steel motor cases returned by rail to Utah, ready to enter later flights. After every ascent, the return began again.

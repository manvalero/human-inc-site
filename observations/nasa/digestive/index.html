NASA

In 2006, with the Space Shuttle approaching retirement, NASA needed a new way to carry what the International Space Station required. The investment went to commercial partners, who also brought capital of their own. Payment followed each completed milestone. One agreement ended before its full award was released. Funding continued as the remaining milestones were completed. By 2013, the combined investment had taken form in two systems capable of carrying cargo to the station.

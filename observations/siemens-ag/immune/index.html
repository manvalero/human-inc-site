Immune
Siemens AG

In 2012, a researcher demonstrated that the private key of a Certificate Authority installed on Siemens S7-1200 V2 controllers could be obtained. Once trusted, that authority caused browsers to trust other S7-1200 V2 controllers. With the key, forged certificates could appear trusted. Siemens told users to remove the authority from browser trust stores and verify each controller individually. Each controller had a device-specific certificate fingerprint. What belonged could no longer be recognized by the shared mark alone.

Siemens AG

In 2001, Siemens entered a nuclear joint venture with Framatome under a broad non-compete that could extend eight to eleven years beyond Siemens’s loss of joint control in 2009. The European Commission later considered the restriction disproportionate, distinguishing areas where the venture operated with its own products from those where it only resold Siemens products or had not operated. In 2012, the old clause was set aside. The non-compete remained only for specified core activities and only for three years after Siemens lost joint control. Restraint held where it fit.

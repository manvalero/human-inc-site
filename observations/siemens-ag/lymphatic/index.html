Siemens AG

In 2000, Siemens Medical began taking back used medical systems worldwide. Systems fit for further use were cleaned, upgraded, tested, and sold again. Older equipment unsuitable for refurbishment could supply spare parts or be withdrawn from service. In July 2001, Siemens established Refurbished Systems as a dedicated unit. What returned was held long enough to be sorted, then moved on.

Siemens AG

In the mid-1990s, researchers at the University of Pittsburgh worked with CTI PET Systems and Siemens Medical Solutions to combine PET and CT in one scanner. PET provided functional information; CT provided anatomical detail. A prototype installed in 1998 was used on more than 300 cancer patients. Experience from it shaped Biograph, the next-generation PET/CT installed in 2001. To function as one, Biograph required a new patient-handling system and integrated software. What emerged had a form of its own.

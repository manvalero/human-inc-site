Siemens AG

In October 2007, Siemens introduced an amnesty during its corruption investigation. Employees outside senior management who voluntarily disclosed truthful, complete information could be protected from dismissal and Siemens claims for damages. The offer was due to end January 31, 2008. Siemens extended it through February, when applications reached 123. In April, individualized leniency followed, including employees previously ineligible for amnesty and those who had not come forward before it ended. Timing changed the consequence.

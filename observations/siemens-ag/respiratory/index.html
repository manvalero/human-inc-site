Siemens AG

By 2001, Siemens established Dispolok, a locomotive rental pool to respond quickly to demand in Europe’s liberalizing rail market. Operators could hire locomotives for a day, six months, or years, depending on their transport contracts. When returned, locomotives could be assigned to another operator. The exchange did not require a single pace.

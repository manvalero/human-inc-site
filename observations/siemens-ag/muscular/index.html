Muscular
Siemens AG

By 1998, higher yields at Siemens’s Dresden semiconductor plant sent more product downstream than planned. Final testing became the bottleneck. Siemens moved rework to an earlier stage, where it took longer but eased the load on final testing. The change was projected to cut assembly and test cycle time by 12% without added equipment and was implemented for selected products. The cycle shortened when the load shifted.

  • Effective Implementation of Cycle Time Reduction Strategies for Semiconductor Back-End ManufacturingWinter Simulation Conference — original proceedings, 1998

Siemens AG

After BenQ acquired Siemens’s mobile phone business in 2005, BenQ Mobile and its service company Inservio became insolvent the next year. By late October 2006, repairs of Siemens and BenQ branded phones had largely stopped. Warranty repairs resumed November 17. In January, Siemens and BenQ agreed on longer term warranty service through B2X, keeping existing channels. Service tore between claim and repair.

Citigroup

In 2015, Citigroup’s preferred resolution plan would place its parent company in Chapter 11 if Citigroup failed, leaving operating entities outside bankruptcy. After regulators called for stronger legal support for the separation, Citigroup entered a binding Support Agreement in 2017 requiring Citicorp to support operating entities before and during a parent bankruptcy. The burden of failure was structured before it had to be carried.

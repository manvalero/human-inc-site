Citigroup

Five years into Latin America’s debt crisis, Citicorp had roughly $10 billion in exposure to the region. On May 19, 1987, it added $3 billion to loan-loss reserves against that exposure. The debts remained outstanding. They were still owed. Full recovery could no longer be assumed.

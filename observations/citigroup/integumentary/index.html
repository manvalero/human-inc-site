Integumentary
Citigroup

In 1999, Citigroup’s branch ATMs relied on touchscreens. Blind customers said they could not use them independently. Five Talking ATMs were tested in California. Their use led to volume control, audible orientation, improved navigation, and tactile controls. In 2001, Citigroup committed to placing a Talking ATM at every U.S. branch. Contact changed the surface.

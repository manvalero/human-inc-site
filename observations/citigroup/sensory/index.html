Citigroup

In 2002, Citigroup spun off Travelers Property Casualty but retained the red umbrella. Five years later, the red umbrella still evoked insurance, specifically St. Paul Travelers, while Citigroup was already Citi to most clients. Citigroup sold the umbrella back to Travelers and began unifying its businesses under Citi. Perception had placed them.

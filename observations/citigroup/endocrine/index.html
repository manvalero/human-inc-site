Citigroup

Before 1992, Citicorp measured its units primarily by revenue growth. In January 1992, Citicorp changed what counted as success: managers would be judged by their contribution to Citicorp’s overall performance and profits. That year, 15 senior leaders began meeting to improve margins across Citicorp, not just within their own areas. What followed unfolded with a different reference.

Citigroup

During the 2023 refurbishment of Citi Tower in London, more than 20,000 pieces of furniture left the building. Through Waste to Wonder, they went to 12 charities. As the refurbishment continued, more furniture followed; by 2024, over 27,000 pieces had been redistributed. What had served there found use beyond it.

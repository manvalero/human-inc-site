Red Cross

In October 1965, the Twentieth International Conference in Vienna unanimously proclaimed the seven Fundamental Principles of the Red Cross. In a separate resolution, the Conference decided that the Principles would be solemnly read at the opening of every future International Conference. At the 34th International Conference in Geneva in 2024, the opening plenary agenda again provided for a reading of the seven Principles. The conference begins inside the Principles.

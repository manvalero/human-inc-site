Observations

Nervous
Red Cross

In early January 1936, reports from Red Cross personnel in Ethiopia about Italy’s use of poison gas began reaching Geneva. The first reports were recorded at headquarters without prompting further inquiry. By early March, they were more frequent and insistent as the ICRC prepared a Rome delegation. On March 17, an ICRC field representative encountered mustard gas near Korem and reported it to Geneva. The March 24 delegation left for Rome carrying that report and independent testimony. In the mission’s preparatory documents, poison gas received only brief mention. What arrived as signal remained background.

Red Cross

In 1924, the League of Red Cross Societies established 15 Manchester Square in London for nurses attending its international public-health courses. Qualified nurses from different National Societies lived there during their year in London, sharing experience from their countries and discussing common problems. New cohorts followed each year. By 1930, 180 nurses had completed the courses. What each brought to the house became part of what returned home with the others.

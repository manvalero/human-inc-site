Immune
Red Cross

In 2018, about a quarter of people receiving Kenya Red Cross cash aid had no official ID. Without one, they could not receive mobile money directly. In 2021, Kenya Red Cross and IFRC tested DIGID with 300 households. Once approved, participants received a QR credential to verify eligibility, either digital or printed with PIN protection. It verified eligibility for aid, not legal identity. The marker adapted. Recognition held.

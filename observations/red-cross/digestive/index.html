Red Cross

On February 28, 1919, the American Red Cross War Council ended its wartime administration. Its accounts showed a balance of $127 million. $33 million was held by chapters for local work. $53 million was in supplies for existing work. $41 million at headquarters was available for new or unfunded needs. Postwar commitments drew on the available funds. By September, the Red Cross estimated $14.5 million would remain available. What remained would fuel the year ahead.

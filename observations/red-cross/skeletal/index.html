Red Cross

In 1919, the League of Red Cross Societies was created. Attempts to settle its relationship with the ICRC continued through the 1920s. Proposals in 1926 and 1927 did not coincide. In 1928, the first Statutes of the International Red Cross kept the institutions distinct. The ICRC remained independent with its own statutes; the League remained an association of National Societies. Where their activities met, each accredited a representative to the other. A nine member Standing Commission was created to address differences between them. The Statutes entered into force on October 26, 1928. The parts remained distinct. Their relation took form.

Red Cross

In 2002, the ICRC launched The Missing and brought together specialists from several fields, including forensic science, around the needs of missing people and their families. In February 2003, more than 350 participants met in Geneva. Their recommendations called for forensic standards, protocols, and greater cooperation. In 2004, the ICRC created a Forensic Unit. By 2006, the unit was developing guidelines, training practitioners, supporting field work, and building international forensic networks. Its work later turned toward strengthening local forensic systems. Applying forensic science to humanitarian problems gave rise to the discipline of humanitarian forensic action.

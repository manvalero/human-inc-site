Red Cross

In 1972, the ICRC and the League of Red Cross Societies began a broad reappraisal. By 1974, 23 National Societies had been examined in detail, including how outsiders saw the Red Cross. Even informed publics knew little about it, yet held it in high regard. The ICRC was described as universally respected; National Societies as aristocratic, establishment, and close to government. No single image contained it.

Observations

Muscular
Red Cross

On April 8, 1968, the ICRC began regular night relief flights into Biafra. By September, the airlift had increased to six, seven, sometimes nine flights in a night. At the landing field, runway lights came on as an aircraft approached. Two trucks pulled alongside and unloading began. Half an hour later, the aircraft was airborne again. The field went dark and the sequence repeated. On February 21–22, Red Cross aircraft touched down eleven times in one night. By March 15, the ICRC had completed 1,000 night flights. In April 1969, after refinements in flying technique and ground coordination, twenty flights were completed in one night. The gain was in the turnaround.

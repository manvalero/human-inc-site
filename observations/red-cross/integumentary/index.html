Red Cross

In 1936, as the Spanish Civil War divided Spain into two zones, postal traffic between them was suspended, leaving many families without contact. The ICRC devised a family message card limited to 25 words of strictly personal news to reduce censorship delays. At its Barcelona office, people could dictate messages, sign them, have incoming ones read aloud, and reply on the reverse. By the end of the war, the service had handled more than five million family messages. The limit kept contact open.

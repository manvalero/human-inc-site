SONY

In 2012, Sony introduced One Sony, placing Hirai at the center of a management team to accelerate decision making across the Group. Sony retained the unified approach in 2013. By 2014, it said the television business needed a more dynamic structure and separated it. The following year, greater business autonomy became Group strategy, again to quicken response. Decision kept moving with responsibility.

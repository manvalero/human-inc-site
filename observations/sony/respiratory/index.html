SONY

In 1994, Sony launched PlayStation on CD-ROM while cartridges remained the standard game format. When a title sold out, retailers could wait several months for new stock. Through Sony Music, Sony already had a CD production and distribution system. Even a small PlayStation game order could move through production to fulfillment in no more than six days. Delivery could arrive while demand still held.

SONY

A young employee told Sony cofounder Akio Morita that he wanted to stay at Sony but could not continue under his section chief. In 1966, Sony introduced its internal job-posting system. Openings appeared in the company newspaper, and employees could apply confidentially for different work. More than 8,000 employees have since changed positions through internal postings across Sony. Movement replenished Sony from within.

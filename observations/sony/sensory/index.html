SONY

In 1979, Sony launched Walkman in Japan. Overseas teams objected to the name because it was not proper English. Abroad, it appeared as Soundabout in the United States, Stowaway in Britain, and Freestyle in Australia. Travelers carried Walkmans abroad, and customers began asking retailers for Walkman by name. In April 1980, Sony made Walkman the worldwide name. Recognition had converged before the name did.

SONY

In 2007, Sony committed ¥60 billion to expand CMOS image sensor production as demand grew. A year later, they remained a priority in its wider investment plan. That December, the downturn forced a review. Sony outsourced part of the planned capacity increase and cut planned electronics investment by roughly 30%. As demand strengthened in 2010, investment returned in stages: ¥40 billion, then another ¥100 billion, into image sensor capacity. Investment narrowed before it widened again.

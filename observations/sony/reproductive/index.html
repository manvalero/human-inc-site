SONY

In 1975, Sony introduced Betamax. Two years later, it began developing a smaller video format. Betamax and VHS were incompatible. Drawing on that experience, Sony announced its 8mm VideoMovie in 1980 and invited other manufacturers to establish a common format for what would follow. In 1982, 122 companies joined the effort. The 8mm standard was finalized in 1984. Sony’s first 8mm camcorder followed in 1985. What Betamax left unresolved entered the next beginning.

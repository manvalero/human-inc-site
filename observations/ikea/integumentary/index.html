IKEA

In the early 1950s, intense price competition in furniture mail order brought IKEA increasing complaints and returns. Customers could read descriptions in the catalogue but could not examine the furniture before ordering. On 30 March 1953, IKEA opened a permanent showroom in a former joinery in Älmhult. Visitors could see, touch, and try the furniture for themselves. What had been promised at a distance could now be tested through contact.

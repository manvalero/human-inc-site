IKEA

In August 2023, a €5.4 billion loan used to acquire IKEA’s proprietary rights was extinguished through a shareholder capital contribution. The rights remained within Inter IKEA Group. The loan’s €324 million annual interest expense ceased. What had entered through borrowing remained after the borrowing itself was transformed into capital.

IKEA

In 1975, IKEA engineer Arne Hall visited Romanian factories producing several similar bookcases that could not be combined. There, he conceived the idea for BILLY. Back in Sweden, Gillis Lundgren sketched Hall’s idea. BILLY appeared in the 1979 IKEA catalogue alongside its predecessor TIGA. TIGA was discontinued in 1981. BILLY continued developing, gaining new configurations and glass doors in 1993. In 2022, redesigned materials and snap fittings made disassembly and reassembly possible. The new model became an origin of its own.

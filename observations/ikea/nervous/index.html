IKEA

IKEA entered Japan in 1974 carrying assumptions that had worked elsewhere. The company withdrew twelve years later. Before returning in 2006, Japanese homes, habits, and ways of living had become signals leadership had learned to interpret. The response changed. IKEA remained one.

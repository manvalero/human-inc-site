IKEA

In 1953, IKEA welcomed visitors to its Älmhult showroom with coffee and biscuits. When the first store opened in 1958, it had a coffee corner. Customers still left at lunchtime to eat elsewhere, interrupting their shopping. In summer 1960, IKEA opened IKEA Baren, its first restaurant, allowing visitors to eat without leaving the store. The pause became part of the exchange.

IKEA

As IKEA introduced its AI customer-service assistant Billie in 2021, Ingka Group began training call-centre co-workers for interior-design advisory roles. By June 2023, 8,500 had received that training, while Billie was handling 47% of customer queries to call centres. New capabilities were being developed within the existing workforce. As work was displaced, support continued to reach the same people.

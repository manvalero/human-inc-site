ROLEX

Rolex was registered in 1908, when British retailers traditionally put their own names on watch dials and resisted displaying the supplier’s name. Hans Wilsdorf began by placing Rolex on one watch in every six. Later, the name appeared on two, then three, four, and eventually five of every six watches. The Oyster was introduced in 1926. By 1927, Rolex announced that every watch leaving its works would carry the name on its dial, case and movement. Presence became constant.

ROLEX

In 2008, Rolex closed a record year but expected lower sales in 2009 and 2010. Rolex reported zero debt and continued with a planned Bienne investment initially valued at CHF 250 million. By September 2009, the project had been reduced to about CHF 200 million. A proposed 50 metre tower was dropped, existing buildings were retained, and the project continued. The commitment came into proportion.

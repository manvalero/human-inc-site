ROLEX

Rolex’s manufacture of gold components leaves offcuts, filings and dust. Clean offcuts return directly to its foundry. Filings mixed with oil are cleaned before refining, while gold caught in polishing cloths and brushes is recovered through incineration. Gold dust is also collected from the resulting fumes. The gold returns to watch production. In 2023, recovered material accounted for 70% of Rolex’s gold supply. Residue remained useful.

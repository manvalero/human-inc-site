ROLEX

In 2022, Rolex introduced its Certified Pre-Owned programme at selected Bucherer locations. Watches entering the programme return to Rolex workshops, where their original configuration is rigorously verified. Each watch is fully disassembled. Every component is individually checked and authenticated. Where necessary, components are replaced with genuine Rolex parts. Only after this process does the watch receive a guarantee card certifying its authenticity. Identity preserved through change.

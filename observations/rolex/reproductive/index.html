ROLEX

In 1926, Rolex introduced the Oyster. Its hermetically sealed case protected the movement from water and dust with a screw down bezel, case back and winding crown. Five years later, the Perpetual rotor joined the Oyster, freeing the wearer from manual winding and forming the Oyster Perpetual. The Oyster Perpetual Datejust followed in 1945, becoming the first self winding waterproof chronometer wristwatch with the date displayed in a window at 3 o’clock. Eleven years later, a new mechanism made the date change instantaneous. Developed for the Datejust, it was later extended across Rolex movements with a date function. Distinction accumulating through continuance.

ROLEX

In 1945, Hans Wilsdorf established the Hans Wilsdorf Foundation to secure the future of Rolex beyond his lifetime. For the next fifteen years, Wilsdorf remained Rolex’s managing director and a board member, until his death in 1960. His shares then passed to the Foundation. Today, the Foundation is the sole shareholder of Rolex Holding SA, with members of its board linking the Foundation to Rolex’s governance. The condition settled while its source was still present.

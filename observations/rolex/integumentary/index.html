ROLEX

Rolex World Service receives watches through its network of Official Rolex Retailers and affiliates. Rolex guarantees the availability of parts and labour for at least 35 years after a model is withdrawn from its catalogue. Exceptional historic watches requiring specific expertise are serviced at the Restoration Atelier in Geneva. There, unavailable components can be recreated using period techniques while preserving as much as possible of the watch’s original characteristics. Time changed what care required.

ROLEX

For the Land-Dweller of 2025, Rolex assembled its new Dynapulse escapement on an external support. There, oil rather than grease was applied in nanolitre quantities through a curved needle. The escapement was then inserted into the movement. Dynapulse had no traditional pallet stone adjustment, so a new diagnostic system checked its functioning in each assembled movement before it was fitted into the watch case. Control was built into the sequence.

Wikipedia

In 2013, Wikimedia stewards proposed a global review of advanced rights holders with no edits or administrative actions for two years. Consultation clarified the threshold: two years of inactivity without community review. The first review examined 844 public wikis. Stewards did not intervene where active local review processes, arbitration committees or special exemptions applied. On other eligible wikis without local review, the global process applied. Inactive rights holders were notified and communities given time to respond. Communities could retain the rights or conduct their own review; without a suitable response, stewards could refer the matter back locally or remove the rights. The global condition entered only where no local one existed.

Wikipedia

In April 2016, Wikimedia shifted its wikis from its Virginia data center to Texas in a full failover exercise. The first switchover took six months to prepare and left the wikis read only for 45 minutes. In 2017, the read only period fell to 17 minutes. By June 2021, it was 1 minute 57 seconds, with more services included. In 2023, Wikimedia made the switchovers recurring every six months, alternating Virginia and Texas as primary. The transfer had become a practiced motion.

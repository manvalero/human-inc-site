Human Inc.
Observations

Immune
Wikipedia

In 2013, Wikimedia research found good faith newcomers increasingly seeing their work rejected by automated and semi automated quality control tools. In 2015, Wikimedia launched ORES, separating edit damage from editor intent. Later testing found ORES scored anonymous and newer editors more harshly. Wikimedia revised the models, but later analyses still found bias. Protection kept learning what not to exclude.

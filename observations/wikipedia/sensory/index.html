Wikipedia

In 2015, Wikimedia standardized what counted as a pageview, its principal measure of web traffic. A pageview required a request for a Wikipedia page. Search could present Wikipedia information without producing one. In 2019, Wikimedia began formally studying that gap. In 2021, Wikimedia and DuckDuckGo tested Wikipedia’s presentation in search. When the information module was removed, 95 percent of the clicks that would have gone to it shifted to ordinary Wikipedia links. Orientation had to be renewed.

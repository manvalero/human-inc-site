Wikipedia

Wikipedia’s text license made reuse cumbersome and was incompatible with a widely used Creative Commons license. Because many contributors held copyright, Wikimedia could not simply relicense the accumulated text. In 2007, Wikimedia asked the Free Software Foundation to revise the existing license. The 2008 revision created a narrow, time limited path for collaborative wikis to adopt Creative Commons licensing. Wikimedia used that provision in 2009. Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike became the primary text license; the earlier license remained secondary. Bearing depended on enough flexibility.

Wikipedia

By 2007, Portuguese and German Wikipedia communities had created local support for newcomers. On February 27, 2012, English Wikipedia opened the Teahouse with experienced editors as hosts. New editors were actively invited in. In its first three months, hosts sent 7,339 invitations. Of 586 participants, most were new editors. When manual outreach became difficult to sustain, automated invitations were introduced while experienced editors kept answering questions. In October 2022, Wikimedia deployed its structured mentor list across wikis using Growth mentorship. Support kept finding new ways to reach.

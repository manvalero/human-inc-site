Wikipedia

On October 4, 2011, contributors to the Italian language Wikipedia replaced its articles with a statement opposing proposed legislation. The Wikimedia Foundation could restore access. It did not. Executive Director Sue Gardner said the Italian Wikipedians understood the proposed law’s implications for their project, and that the Foundation trusted their judgment. Two days later, Italian Wikipedia restored its articles. The override stayed unused. The local signal led.

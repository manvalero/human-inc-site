2026 FIFA World Cup

During the 2026 FIFA World Cup, researchers at Gensler and ROAR examined hundreds of thousands of fan posts connected to the sixteen host stadiums. Across every venue, posts from surrounding public spaces used “we,” “our,” and “us” 70% more often than posts from inside the stadiums. When posts contained words associated with awe or wonder, collective language appeared 189% more often, regardless of location. The gathering had another voice just beyond the stands.

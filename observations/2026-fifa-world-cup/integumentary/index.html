2026 FIFA World Cup

Before the 2026 FIFA World Cup, stadium rules allowed spectators to bring empty, transparent reusable water bottles of up to one litre. On June 4, FIFA withdrew that allowance, citing the risk of bottles being thrown. Supporters objected, raising concerns over drinking water access during summer heat. The next day, FIFA revised the rules for matches in the United States and Canada. Each spectator could bring one factory-sealed, soft-plastic disposable water bottle of up to 590 ml. Reusable bottles remained prohibited.

2026 FIFA World Cup

During the 2026 FIFA World Cup, FIFA's Social Media Protection Service examined more than 17 million posts and comments concerning monitored players, coaches and match officials. Automated screening referred 477,217 for human review. Analysts verified 240,676 as abusive and reported them to social media platforms. At the account level, 232 serious cases were prioritized for investigation. Further assessment of sender locations and other circumstances led to 145 being de-escalated. The remaining 87 underwent deeper investigation. Thirty accounts were positively identified for action.

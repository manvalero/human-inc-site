2026 FIFA World Cup

The 2026 FIFA World Cup expanded to forty-eight teams, adding another knockout round. Under previous tournament rules, single yellow cards were cleared only after the quarter-finals. To accommodate the added round, FIFA approved an amendment on April 28. Single cautions would now be cleared after the group stage and again after the quarter-finals. Two cautions in different matches before a clearance still triggered an automatic one-match suspension. A player entering the round of 32 with a single group-stage caution would no longer carry it forward.

2026 FIFA World Cup

For the 2022 World Cup, FIFA’s $209 million Club Benefits Programme compensated clubs linked to players who reached the tournament. Qualifying appearances alone generated no payment. For 2026, FIFA increased the fund to $355 million and established two calculations: $100 million for qualifying appearances, measured per player per match, and $250 million for World Cup participation, measured per player per day. Another $5 million remained reserved. A national team could finish its journey in qualification. Its clubs’ contribution no longer needed a later result to count.

Oxford University

In medieval Oxford, masters actively teaching in the University also took part in governing it. By the early twentieth century, resident teachers and administrators responsible for much of Oxford’s work held too little influence in its governance. From 1926, Oxford widened Congregation’s participation. Decades later, its influence narrowed again. In 2000, a new Council remained answerable to Congregation. Eight centuries later, the governing condition still included those engaged in Oxford’s academic life.

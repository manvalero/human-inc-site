Oxford University

In 1959, research on high-field magnets in Oxford Physics led to Oxford Instruments. Research begun inside the University could now continue beyond the laboratory in an independent form. Oxford later developed ways for other research to do the same. Between 2011 and 2023, Oxford created 205 spinouts; in 2023, it marked its 300th such organization. What 1959 first revealed was still unfolding decades later.

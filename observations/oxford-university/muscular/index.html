Observations

Muscular
Oxford University

In November 2010, Oxford began retrieving books from the new Bodleian Book Storage Facility once a day. The load grew. A second shift was added in February. Each day, 1,000 boxes left storage in Cheshire. They were barcoded that day and moved to the new facility the next day. By late July, 5.3 million items had entered—nearly 140,000 each week. By August, two operational teams were retrieving books five times through the day. Each new load met a movement further practiced.

Oxford University

After the Second World War, Oxford’s academic work expanded beyond the fellowship available through its colleges. By 1962, 47% of its full-time senior members of Congregation were not college Fellows. They were already teaching and researching across Oxford. In 1965, St Cross and Iffley opened, bringing college fellowship to academics who had remained outside it. Fellowship began reaching as far as Oxford’s academic body had grown.

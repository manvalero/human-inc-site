Human Inc.
Observations

Sensory
Oxford University

In 2009, the Pitt Rivers Museum reopened with a new entrance. Visitor numbers rose sharply, and satisfaction reached 99 percent. The Museum kept looking. In 2010–11, a closer reading of the visitors’ book revealed that many people enjoyed the Museum while seeing its displays as extraordinary spectacle and taking away messages different from those expected. From 2012 to 2017, VERVE kept observing through visitor research, interviews, testing, feedback, and attention to movement through the displays. By 2017, the Museum was seeing itself through its visitors.

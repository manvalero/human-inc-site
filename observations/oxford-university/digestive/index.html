Oxford University

In 1249, William of Durham left Oxford 310 marks to be converted into annual rents supporting ten or more Masters of Arts studying theology. Oxford purchased some rents. Part of the remaining fund was used for University business and the rest lent elsewhere. None of those loans returned. By 1280, the remaining income could sustain only four masters. Only what had been absorbed could continue to sustain them.

Integumentary
Oxford University

The entrance passageway of what became St John’s College had opened onto St Giles since the late fifteenth century. Behind the threshold, the Lodge took on layers from different periods. By 2020, the entrance was no longer meeting everyone who arrived there with the same ease. Wheelchair users could not access the College through the main gate, while the Lodge received one visitor at a time, leaving others in the passage. St John’s created level access from St Giles and enlarged the Lodge, allowing more people to approach through the College’s principal entrance. An old edge had recovered its receptivity.

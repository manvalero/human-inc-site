Observations

Immune
Oxford University

In November 2015, Dridex malware caused considerable disruption across Oxford. The attack revealed a pattern in susceptibility. Seventy percent of those who had fallen for it had not completed the University’s security-awareness training. Dridex became a focus of Oxford’s information-security work. Two months later, a variant of the same malware returned with little impact. The first encounter had changed what the system could recognize.

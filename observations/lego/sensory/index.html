LEGO

Adult builders had been organizing independently for years. In 2000, BrickLink gave that growing community an online place to connect. LEGO began paying closer attention to the creativity emerging among adult fans. In 2007, it introduced Modular Buildings as a line aimed specifically at Adult Fans of LEGO. Presence was already there before it was recognized.

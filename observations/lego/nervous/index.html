Nervous
LEGO

By 2003, there was very little understanding inside LEGO of how major parts of the company related to one another. That year, its executive leadership team was reduced from fourteen to nine. In 2004, LEGO changed how direction and information were transmitted. Leadership held more than 45 town halls around the world where employees could question them directly. The intranet provided daily updates and direct access to leaders at fixed times. The new pathways left more room for understanding to emerge.

LEGO

In 2017, LEGO launched LEGO Life, a social network for children under 13 designed to keep identifying information out. Uploads were screened before publication. Children began submitting selfies. The images did not pass the filter. Some kids covered their faces with LEGO minifigure stickers. The workaround forced LEGO to reconsider what could still identify them. The altered selfies were also judged too identifying. The attempt to pass the filter sharpened the distinction.

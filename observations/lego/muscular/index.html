Muscular
LEGO

In 1947, LEGO acquired its first injection-molding machine. Considerable experimentation was needed as the company learned the process. Molding became established over the following years. From 1961, what experiments and calculations revealed became principles for greater control. In 1963, LEGO began systematic molding training for supervisors. Cycles shortened while required quality held. What had once required experimentation was now able to be repeated with precision.
